Why it's fast

Three ideas the mainstream clients don't fully exploit

Not micro-optimisation — architecture. Each one is measured in the benchmark log with reproducible commands.

🔁

Pipelined NNTP

Several article requests stay in flight per connection, so round-trips never idle the socket — and TCP never decays its congestion window between articles. Measured +12% to +270% over serial at four sites; a line saturates with 8 connections instead of 30–50.

🚿

The one-pass pipeline

Articles are SIMD-decoded in place, written once at their final offsets, PAR2-verified from the decode buffers, and store-mode RAR contents are extracted during the download — the volumes never exist as files. Disk writes = content × 1.0.

🕸️

Union availability

An article is only "missing" when every configured backbone has refused it. Block-exact accounting decides COMPLETE / REPAIRABLE / IMPOSSIBLE before wasting bytes, fetches the exact-fit minimum of recovery blocks, and aborts hopeless downloads in seconds.

The pipeline

Touch every byte once

Competitors write the archive volumes, read them back to verify, read them again to extract, then write the output — roughly 2× writes and 2× reads. nzbfast does this instead:

socket ──TLS──▶ rapidyenc SIMD decode (in place — 4.95 GB/s per core)
                   │
                   ├─▶ pwrite at final offset          (plain posts)
                   │      └─ or: RAR-map translate ─▶ pwrite straight into the
                   │         extracted file (store-mode posts — the rar
                   │         volumes never touch disk)
                   ├─▶ PAR2 block hasher — every block MD5-verified from the
                   │      decode buffer; verification finishes with the download
                   └─▶ availability ledger — block-exact health, live
Damaged post? Bad blocks are known the moment their articles fail, the exact-fit minimum of recovery volumes is fetched during the download (22 blocks fetched for 20 needed, in the bench round), and repair touches only the damaged spans. Impossible post? It aborts in seconds having downloaded almost nothing — before your quota notices.

Headline features

Everything a serious setup needs

The full list — every knob, every integration — is on the features page.

  • Line-rate downloads — pipelined NNTP measured at 8.31 Gbps on 10 GbE; the fastest client on every clean benchmark leg, at every size.
  • One-pass pipeline — decode, verify and extract overlap; store-mode RAR volumes never hit disk; needs 1× disk where others need ~2×.
  • Multi-provider union availability — block-exact routing across backbones; preflight verdicts; exact-fit repair; early abort of impossible posts.
  • Memory budget — one global RAM budget with graceful spill tiers. It never swaps your machine: a 190 GB job completes inside a 1 GB budget (~1.1 GB peak RSS).
  • Crash-safe resume — an article-level journal means a crash or kill −9 mid-download costs almost nothing: it resumes, refetching only what wasn't saved.
  • Drop-in APIs — SABnzbd-compatible API and NZBGet JSON-RPC: Sonarr, Radarr, nzb360 and LunaSea work unmodified. One-click import of your SAB/NZBGet config.
  • Built-in indexer — scans your newsgroups into a searchable local index, serves Newznab so your *arr stack can search it too.
  • Poster wall with keyless metadata — artwork, ratings, cast and synopses for everything in your index, with no API keys to sign up for.
  • Watchlist automation — name a show or film (aired or not); it's grabbed the moment it appears, then quality-upgraded until your target is met.
  • A single executable — repair (par2) and unpack (RAR) ride inside the binary. Unzip, run, done.

The showcase

Your index, presented as a poster wall

The built-in indexer scans the newsgroups you care about; the wall turns the result into posters, ratings, cast and episode grids — using only keyless public sources, fetched by your own install. Click an episode chip to download it.

Fits your stack

Point your existing tools at it

Sonarr / Radarr

Full SABnzbd-compatible API — add nzbfast as a "SABnzbd" download client and it just works: grabs, categories, priorities, retries, post-processing scripts (SAB_* contract), history. The Newznab facade also lets the *arrs search your local index as if it were an indexer.

nzb360 / LunaSea

The daemon speaks NZBGet's JSON-RPC too — the popular phone remotes drive nzbfast unmodified. And the dashboard itself has a first-class phone layout for when you're away from the big screen.

Coming from SAB or NZBGet?

One click imports your servers from an existing SABnzbd or NZBGet install (it also reads sabnzbd.ini directly). Watch folders, RSS feeds with filters, smart folders with TV filing, and cleanup rules come along for the ride.

Try it in the next two minutes

Unzip, double-click the launcher, answer three questions (or let it find your SABnzbd config), and the dashboard opens. First download inside two minutes.
